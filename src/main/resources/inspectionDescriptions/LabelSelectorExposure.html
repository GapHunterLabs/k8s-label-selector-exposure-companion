Reports a Deployment, Pod, StatefulSet or DaemonSet that a Service exposes but that no NetworkPolicy with ingress rules selects, in a project that does define NetworkPolicies.

The namespace looks protected, but this workload falls outside every policy and accepts any ingress traffic. Selectors are resolved across all YAML manifests of the project with Kubernetes semantics: matchLabels and matchExpressions with In, NotIn and Exists.
